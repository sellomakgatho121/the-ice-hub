# The Ice Hub — Reference Research

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Purpose:** redesign pass; direction was set (cold, slow, mineral) but visual translation was open.

## References

### 1. Apple — iPhone product page (apple.com/iphone)
- Accessed: 2026-10-25
- What it does well: one subject per scroll stage, scroll-paced reveals, near-zero chrome, photography carries the story.
- What to learn: pace the hero so the product is the only thing moving; hold still between beats.
- What NOT to carry over: Apple's photography, brand voice, exact grid, "Buy" CTA styling.

### 2. Linear (linear.app)
- Accessed: 2026-10-25
- What it does well: executed minimalism, hairline rules, dense but readable, fast.
- What to learn: restraint as a feature; one accent; tabular data.
- What NOT to carry over: their purple accent, marketing copy, SaaS section sequence.

### 3. Aesop (aesop.com)
- Accessed: 2026-10-25
- What it does well: editorial, type-led, generous whitespace, single accent, tactile without texture.
- What to learn: type carries hierarchy when imagery is absent; whitespace is content.
- What NOT to carry over: their serif pairing, product photography, brand voice.

### 4. Stripe (stripe.com)
- Accessed: 2026-10-25
- What it does well: contrast discipline, clear data presentation, no gradient text.
- What to learn: tabular numerals, clear focus states, restrained motion.
- What NOT to carry over: their blue, hero gradient, developer-focused copy.

### 5. 60fps.design (60fps.design)
- Accessed: 2026-10-25
- What it does well: 60fps mobile-first interaction patterns, spring physics, scroll snap, bottom sheets, thumb-zone CTAs.
- What to learn: mobile-centric layout, gesture-driven navigation, spring easing, no hover-only affordances.
- What NOT to carry over: the app aesthetic, specific branded animations, any third-party library.

### 6. Serkova (serkova.com)
- Accessed: 2026-10-25. WordPress, Greek spirits brand; template uses fullPage.js-style full-viewport parallax sections, video backgrounds, count-up stats, transparent menu, and a slider counter.
- What it does well: each section is a full-viewport stage; scroll is the edit. One subject per screen, large display type, dark cinematic scrims, a single warm accent against near-black.
- What to learn: full-viewport pacing, parallax depth, count-up proof points, transparent-to-solid header, one CTA per stage.
- What NOT to copy: its red/gold brand palette, PF Handbook Pro (commercial licence, not available), Advent Pro, fullPage.js dependency, video assets, Greek copy, section order, or literal product photography.
- Applied: parallax story band, count-up stats, fixed transparent header, hero CTA. Not applied: scroll-snap hijacking (conflicts with the sticky scrub hero), fullPage.js.

All six are craft references, not measured evidence. No conversion or CWV data cited.
Popular ≠ appropriate; chosen for principles (restraint, pace, type-led hierarchy), not identity.
